Hi, welcome to my website, I am Hasan Helal. I'm a senior undergraduate in physics at UCLA, working with Dr. Schaeffer in the [Schaeffer Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/derekschaeffer/home) on laboratory plasma astrophysics. My work focuses on numerical modelling of magnetized collisionless shocks using particle-in-cell simulations. My role has included: building new tools to model shocks in PIC, building synthetic diagnostics to compare with experiment and benchmarking with existing solvers and analytical theory. The goal is to fill in the gap between our understanding of the kinetic features in shocks from experiments with computational techniques. 

I hope to continue my work and work through my degree at UCLA. My dream has always been to pursue graduate school doing research in theory/computational physics. On my own I like to build numerical "toy models" to help me visualize and intuit concepts in theoretical physics. My passion comes from my curiosity so these projects range in subject from quantum mechanics to fluid mechanics. If you wish to contact me, I prefer using my email: hasanh429@g.ucla.edu.
